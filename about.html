Our Story

Founded in 2024, Coffee with Yvie started as a passion project to bring people together over the perfect cup of coffee.

We source our beans from sustainable farms around the world, roasting them in-house to ensure the freshest flavor in every cup.

Our 3D digital experience is just the beginning. We believe in blending tradition with technology to create a unique cafe atmosphere.
